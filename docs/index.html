EA-TDC

Treasury component of deposits: estimates and transmission.

This work asks how the baseline estimate of the Treasury component of deposits moves deposits first, how the remaining deposit component adjusts, and how much of that adjustment can be reconstructed with a simple accounting breakdown.

Questions

Research questions.

The central questions are whether the Treasury component of deposits first shows up in deposits, whether that result survives broader holder definitions, and whether the non-TDC deposit component can be reconstructed in a sensible way.

TDC definition

Equations and notation.

View equations

The baseline treatment is the marketable-Treasury, transaction-based estimate of the Treasury component of deposits. In the upstream estimator its short name includes "bank" because it excludes credit unions, not because it excludes the rest of world. The actual baseline formula below includes Federal Reserve, bank-sector, and rest-of-world Treasury transactions.

Headline estimator

Baseline TDC estimate used in the quarterly results

This is the empirical TDC estimate used in the main quarterly work. It includes Federal Reserve, bank-sector, and rest-of-world Treasury transactions, subtracts Treasury operating cash, and adds positive Federal Reserve remittances.

\[\widehat{\Delta D}^{mkt,bank}_{TDC,t} = \left(\Delta TS^{tx}_{Fed,t} + \Delta TS^{tx}_{Banks,t} + \Delta TS^{tx}_{ROW,t}\right) - \Delta Cash^{tx}_{Treasury,t} + Remit^{+}_{Fed,t}\]
ΔD^mkt,bank_TDC,t
Estimated baseline TDC used in period t.
ΔTS^tx_Fed,t
Federal Reserve net transactions in marketable Treasury securities.
ΔTS^tx_Banks,t
Bank-sector net transactions in marketable Treasury securities.
ΔTS^tx_ROW,t
Rest-of-world net transactions in marketable Treasury securities.
ΔCash^tx_Treasury,t
Change in Treasury operating cash transactions; higher Treasury cash drains deposits.
Remit^+_Fed,t
Positive Federal Reserve remittances to Treasury, summed within the period.
Theory identity 1

Deposit-user flow identity

TDC can be read as Treasury injections into deposit users plus net Treasury-security redistribution toward reserve users.

\[\Delta D_{TDC} = \left(G_{dep} - T_{dep}\right) + \left(D_{sales} - D_{purch}\right) + D_{yield}\]
ΔD_TDC
Treasury-attributed change in deposits.
G_dep
Treasury spending paid to agents that hold deposits.
T_dep
Taxes and other Treasury receipts collected from deposit-holding agents.
D_sales
Deposit inflow created when Treasury securities are sold into deposit-holding sectors.
D_purch
Deposit outflow created when deposit-holding sectors buy Treasury securities.
D_yield
Net Treasury interest and related cash-flow support to deposits.
Theory identity 2

Reserve-user flow identity

The same object can be re-expressed through Treasury issuance, reserve-user cash flows, positive Fed remittances, and the Treasury cash balance.

\[\Delta D_{TDC} = \left(D_{sales} - D_{purch}\right) + \left(T_{AV} - T_{Rx} - R_{yield} + R_{Tx}\right) + \max(0, F_{PY} + M_{MT} - F_{OE}) - \Delta TGA\]
T_AV
Treasury issuance absorbed by reserve-using sectors.
T_Rx
Treasury receipts paid by reserve-using sectors.
R_yield
Yield flows paid out by reserve users on the Treasury side of the balance sheet.
R_Tx
Other Treasury-related transfers that move cash back toward deposit users.
F_PY
Federal Reserve income available for remittance.
M_MT
Maturity-related Treasury cash support that reaches Treasury from the Fed side.
F_OE
Federal Reserve operating expenses and other deductions from remittable income.
ΔTGA
Change in the Treasury General Account balance.
Theory identity 3

Money-balance decomposition

A rough decomposition view reads TDC as the part of money growth left after stripping out non-deposit money and non-Treasury balance-sheet channels.

\[\Delta D_{TDC} = (\Delta M - \Delta C - \Delta X) - (\Delta L_{B,DU} + \Delta S_{B,DU} - \Delta CB_B) - \Delta CB_{NB} - \Delta FI_{NonTS}\]
ΔM
Broad money growth.
ΔC
Currency growth outside deposits.
ΔX
Other money-like components excluded from deposits.
ΔL_B,DU
Bank lending to deposit users.
ΔS_B,DU
Bank purchases of private securities from deposit users.
ΔCB_B
Central-bank liquidity support flowing through banks.
ΔCB_NB
Central-bank liquidity support flowing through nonbanks.
ΔFI_NonTS
Non-Treasury financial-asset channel that also moves deposits.

TDC means the Treasury-attributed component of deposits: the part of deposit change traced to Treasury cash operations, Treasury security transactions, and related official-balance-sheet channels.
